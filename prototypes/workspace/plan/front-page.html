  1. 1
    Top bar

    Logo and a way back to all topics. Stays pinned while you scroll.

  2. 2
    Animated sky hero

    Particles rise in a glowing thermal and a glider circles in it. Sets the mood in the first second.

  3. 3
    Search

    Finds topics as you type and shows up to six matches.

  4. 4
    Stat counters

    Questions, guests and drawings, each with its own icon.

  5. 5
    Subject tiles

    Seven subjects, each with a round orange icon that bobs when you hover.

  6. 6
    Featured article

    A looping animation of the wing collapsing and reopening, beside the title and link.

  7. 7
    Did you know

    A glowing bulb and a new fact every 7 seconds, from guests and sources. Arrows to flip through.

  8. 8
    A to Z topics

    Letter badges. Orange means the article is ready; grey ones show a placeholder.
