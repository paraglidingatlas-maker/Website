Nothing on this site or in the podcast is a substitute for training. Everything here is educational. If you are deciding whether to do something in the air, the people to ask are your instructor and your national association, not a podcast.

1. Is Paragliding Dangerous?

Paragliding carries inherent risks, as any aerial sport does. Following proper safety protocols, getting certified training and using appropriate equipment reduces those risks significantly. Our discussions are for informational purposes only and do not replace proper training.

2. Should I Try The Techniques Discussed In The Podcast?

Never attempt any technique discussed in the podcast without proper training and supervision from certified instructors. Everything shared is for educational purposes.

3. Are Your Recommendations Official Safety Guidelines?

No. We work hard to be accurate, but nothing here should be treated as official guidance. Always refer to your local paragliding association, certified instructors and equipment manufacturers.

4. Do You Accept Liability For Podcast Information?

No. The information in our podcast is for entertainment and educational purposes. We do not accept liability for actions taken on the basis of podcast content. Seek professional instruction and follow your local regulations.

5. How Do I Verify Safety Information?

Check anything that matters against:

  • Your certified paragliding instructor
  • Your local paragliding association
  • The equipment manufacturer
  • The official aviation authority in your region

6. What Are Your Affiliate Relationships?

We may use affiliate links when recommending products or services, which means we could earn a commission if you buy through them, at no additional cost to you. All affiliate relationships are disclosed in the episode description.

7. Do You Accept Sponsored Content?

Yes, occasionally. Sponsored episodes or segments are clearly marked and disclosed at the start of the episode. We only partner with brands and products we believe in and that align with our values.

8. How Do You Handle Product Reviews?

When we review a product:

  • We state clearly if it was provided free
  • We keep editorial independence
  • We give honest, unbiased opinions
  • We disclose any relationship with the manufacturer

9. What Is Your Content Policy?

Our content is original, based on personal experience, expert interviews and research. We aim for accuracy, but the opinions expressed are our own and are not professional advice.

10. How Do You Handle Listener Privacy?

We respect listener privacy and follow the GDPR. Data collected through this site or our social platforms is handled according to our Privacy Policy. We do not share personal information with third parties without consent.

Something here unclear?

Email aninder@paraglidingatlas.com. If it is a question about your own flying, ask your instructor first; we would rather be second in that queue.
