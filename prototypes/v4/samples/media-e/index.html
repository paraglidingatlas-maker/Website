Questions

For Organisers

Another question? Book a call or send a WhatsApp.

What does it cost?

Every package is priced per event. We agree the price on a call, once I know your event, its dates and what you would like from it.

Can we start small?

Yes. Start with the resources you have and grow the coverage as your budget allows.

When does it come out?

The same day or at the end of the week, as you need. Same-day delivery depends on the internet on site, when the task finishes and access to footage.

Where is it published?

As joint posts on your channels and on Paragliding Atlas, so both audiences see it.

Can you cover a whole season?

Yes. The Season package covers every event of your series, with Race Behind The Race between events.
