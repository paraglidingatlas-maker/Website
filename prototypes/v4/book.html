    What happens to this

    Pressing either button passes your details through a small relay of our own, running on Cloudflare. It keeps the hold under your reference, takes the card payment on Stripe's own page if you pay now, and sends you the confirmation and, two days later, the welcome kit. Your card details never touch our relay or this site.
