The record

Two Records, One Straight Line

The FAI keeps world records for the speed a paraglider can hold around a declared course. On an out-and-return course the pilot flies from the start to a turnpoint and straight back, and the record is the distance divided by the time. I am going for two of them: the 100 km course and the 200 km course, in class O-3, paragliders.
