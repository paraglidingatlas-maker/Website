Origin Story

Flying As A Way Of Seeing The World

Paragliding Atlas began with one tandem flight: mine, as a child, in the foothills of the Himalaya.

Every trip since has been built the same way: small groups, genuine hospitality, and the highest safety standards I can build.

  1. Childhood

    I take my first tandem paragliding flight in the foothills of the Himalayas. The obsession begins.

  2. 2023

    I found Paragliding Atlas, aiming to build immersive flying experiences beyond a quick tandem flight.

  3. Today

    A small team of pilots guiding expeditions across Kenya, the Himalayas, Peru, and Kazakhstan, telling the stories behind them on the Paragliding Atlas podcast.

Aninder Singh

Founder, Paragliding Atlas · host of the podcast
