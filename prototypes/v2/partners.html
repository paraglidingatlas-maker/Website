How I work with sponsors

The trust is the product

Pilots come here because the knowledge is honest. That is what makes a mention here worth something, so I protect it in every partnership.

  1. I clearly mark sponsored episodes and segments and disclose them at the start of the episode.
  2. I only partner with brands and products I believe in and that align with my values.
  3. Editorial independence stays with me. When I review a product, I say if it was provided free and disclose any relationship with the manufacturer.
  4. Affiliate relationships are disclosed in the episode description.
