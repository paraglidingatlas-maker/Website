Origin Story

Flying as a way of seeing the world

At Paragliding Atlas, we believe our sport isn't just about flying. It's about experiencing the world from a perspective few dare to explore: embracing the wind, mastering the skies, and discovering the untold stories of the clouds and the lands beneath them.

Our team is united by a single passion: to turn every flying holiday into a series of unforgettable moments, delivered with genuine hospitality and the highest safety standards we can build.

Flying can feel like love at first sight, and that's exactly what happened to us. Aninder Singh, as a child, took his first tandem flight in the foothills of the Himalayas, and the story for the rest of the team follows a similar shape.

  1. Childhood

    Aninder takes his first tandem paragliding flight in the foothills of the Himalayas. The obsession begins.

  2. 2023

    Aninder founds Paragliding Atlas, aiming to build immersive flying experiences beyond a quick tandem flight.

  3. Today

    A small team of pilots guiding expeditions across Kenya, the Himalayas, Peru, and Kazakhstan, telling the stories behind them on the Paragliding Atlas podcast.
