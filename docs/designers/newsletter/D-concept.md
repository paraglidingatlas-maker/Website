# Newsletter, contest round 1: designer D (Trim), "The Day Sheet"

Mock: `prototypes/v4/samples/newsletter-d/index.html` (shot-390.png, shot-1440.png). Worked alone; E, F and G unseen.

## The idea in one line
The week reported as a race sheet: one true photo and one true sentence per task, every result in live text, and
one button (the photos). Nothing in it you cannot check, and it still reads with images off.

## Subject and preview (two each, no em dashes)
| | Subject | Preview line |
|---|---|---|
| **A (recommended)** | Tapalpa, four tasks in | Four tasks flown, one stopped by rain, one with nobody in goal. The photos and films are up. |
| B | From the media team at SRS Tapalpa | 465 photos free to save, three highlight films and a talk with Miguel Gutiérrez. |

Why A: it names the place and the state of play in four words, and the preview carries the two surprises (rain,
nobody in goal) that make a pilot open it. B leads with the owner's role, which is better once readers know him.
After 14 Nov: "Tapalpa, the final results" / "Seven tasks, [winner] on top. Every photo and film from the week."

## Why it gets read and clicked
- The subject and the strip answer "what happened" before any scrolling; a pilot who follows SRS gets the results
  in the email itself, which earns the open next time.
- Every task row offers the two things people want from a comp (the film, their photo) right beside the story, and
  each link lands on that task, not on a page top.
- One solid button only, so there is no doubt what the email wants; Patreon asks once, after it has given.
- Craft that inboxes punish if missing: all words are live text (Gmail with images off, screen readers, dark mode
  inverters keep it readable); every image is a JPEG on the dark matte with width and height set, so nothing jumps
  and no transparent rim shows; the logo is baked onto the card colour for the same reason; alt text says what the
  photo shows; the HTML is 22 KB, far below Gmail's 102 KB clip.
