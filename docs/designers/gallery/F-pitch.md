# F, Turnpoint: "Fly the Comp", for the owner
1. The comp's days sit on one line at their real dates, like your home page's season line: you see at a glance which days have photos, that today's come tonight, and which days are still to come.
2. A pilot taps their day and sees only that day's photos, in the order you shot them, so the page stays short and quick on hotel wifi; the small glider flies along the line to the day they tap.
3. Tap a photo: it opens big with its file name, "Save, free" (straight to Photos or Instagram on a phone) and "Original, EUR 1" (the payment link is still [to supply]).
4. "Find your wing" colour buttons are there as an optional idea: they dim the other photos and never hide one.
5. It is a sample: the photos are Piedrahita stand-ins, and the page shows the evening of 11 Nov (Task 4 flying), with links to see it before, during and after the comp.
