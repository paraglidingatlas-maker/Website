# Cloudbase (E): "Pull Back to the Comp", the pitch
1. Every day of the comp is on one page, in date order. Today's task shows "photos tonight" and the days to come wait as quiet rows, so a pilot sees at once whether their day is up.
2. One tap on "Scan all" pulls the camera back: every photo of the comp shrinks onto one screen, and a pilot spots their own wing by its colours. Nobody has to tag anything.
3. Tap a photo and it grows out of its place. "Save, free" puts the 2400 px copy straight into the phone's Photos (or Instagram, WhatsApp). "Original, EUR 1" shows the photo's name for the order; the payment link is [to supply].
4. Swipe the photo down and it flies back into its own frame, lit orange for a moment, so you never lose your place among 350.
5. It stays smooth with 350 photos; to keep Scan light the real page needs one small 320 px copy of each photo. Sample: prototypes/v4/samples/gallery-e/ (stand-in photos from Piedrahita; add ?stress for 350).
