# The site in other languages (chat 27, 17 Nov 2026)

German, French, Spanish and Simplified Chinese, at /de/, /fr/, /es/, /zh/. The owner has no human reviewer,
so the pipeline below is the review: nothing is published that fails it. English stays the source of truth.
Never use a Google Translate widget.

## Files
- `tools/v4_i18n.py`: the tool (segments, locked facts, checks, build, report). Its docstring has the commands.
- `tools/data/i18n/config.json`: languages, the pages that are translated (`pages`), the pages whose English
  is binding (`english_binding`, never translated), the strings of the language note (`ui`) and the
  strings the page scripts write (`js`).
- `tools/data/i18n/keep.txt`: names never translated (locked like numbers).
- `tools/data/i18n/glossary.json`: paragliding terms per language, each with its source (DHV, FFVL ...).
  A term with no source is `[to supply]` and is not enforced.
- `tools/data/i18n/<lang>.json`: the translation memory, keyed by a hash of the locked English segment. Each
  record keeps `a` and `b` (the two independent translations), `tr` (the reviewer's pick or merge), `back`
  (the blind back-translation), `check` ("ok", or why not), and any `waive` (a soft check waived, with its
  reason).
- `tools/data/i18n/BRIEF.md`: the brief every translation pass gets.
- `tools/data/i18n/report.md`: the last check report.
- `prototypes/v4/v4-i18n.css`, `v4-i18n.js`: the language menu and note; the runtime dictionary for text
  the page scripts write after load (a countdown, the photo viewer, Share, the form's replies).
- `tools/data/i18n/work/` (gitignored): the passes' working files.

## How a page is made
1. `python3 tools/v4_i18n.py jobs`: cuts the built English pages into segments. Facts (numbers, dates,
   prices, URLs, emails, `keep.txt` names, file names, `[to supply]`) become `{1}`, `{2}`; inline markup
   becomes `<x1>`...`</x1>`. Writes `work/<lang>-todo.json` (what is not in memory yet) and
   `work/all-segments.json` (each with its `facts`).
2. Two independent translators per language (different models), each `validate`d to 0 hard problems:
   `python3 tools/v4_i18n.py validate LANG FILE [TODO]`. Then `ingest LANG a|b FILE`.
3. A reviewer per language picks or merges A and B (glossary binding) into `tr`: `ingest LANG tr FILE`
   (`{"key": {"tr": "...", "note": "...", "waive": {"left in English": "a person's name"}}}`).
4. A back-translator who never sees the English translates `tr` back into English: `ingest LANG back FILE`.
5. A comparer reads English and back-translation side by side: "ok", or what meaning shifted:
   `ingest LANG check FILE`. A flagged segment goes back to step 3, then 4 and 5 again.
6. `python3 tools/v4_i18n.py build` (also the last step of `tools/v4_build.py`): writes every page whose
   segments all pass; a page with a missing, stale or flagged segment is NOT written (and is removed if it
   was), and its English page then does not offer that language. It also marks the English pages
   (hreflang, the language menu).
7. `python3 tools/v4_i18n.py check`: the report (segments per state, facts on every written page, broken
   links). Then the browser check (screenshots at 1440 and 390, no English left, no overflow).

## The checks
Hard (never waived): every `{n}` once and unchanged; markup placeholders whole and nested; no digit that
is not a locked fact; no stray brace; not another language (a mixed-up file: it happened once in the
pilot, a translator's helper script wrote Spanish into the German file, and this check caught it).
Soft (waivable by the reviewer, with a reason): left in English (a name), a country not in its CLDR name,
a glossary term, length far from the English.
Numbers with a separator or decimals and dates are rendered by code in the language's own format (CLDR via
babel; the browser's Intl for dates the scripts write); the facts are never typed by a translator.

## On the site
- `/de/page.html` etc.; `<html lang>`; canonical and og:url to itself; hreflang (en, the languages,
  x-default) on both the English and the translated pages; the language menu in the nav (a globe; the
  icon alone on a phone); the note under the nav: "Translated from English. Read the English page ·
  Report a translation error" (to corrections.html, until the owner names another address).
- `tools/v2_site.py` treats every page under de/ fr/ es/ zh/ as a new page (switched, indexable, in
  sitemap.xml); `tools/v2_switch.py` turns `[to supply]` into the language's "Coming soon".
- `tools/v4_search.py` leaves the translations out of the English search.
- Forms: `<option>` keeps its English value (`data-en`), so the enquiry email stays English and
  `?trip=kenya` still finds its trip.
- Chinese: no web font (system Chinese faces); films still link to YouTube (blocked in mainland China).

## Pages whose English is binding
terms, privacy policy, cookie policy, participant agreement, safety and disclosure: not translated; when
the site links them from a translated page, the note says "The English version is binding."
