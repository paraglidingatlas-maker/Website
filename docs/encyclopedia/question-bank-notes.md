# Question bank: notes

`question-bank.csv` holds 245 questions, sorted by demand: 48 high, 112 medium, 85 low. Within each band, the questions with the broadest evidence come first. 61 rows are flagged SAFETY. 26 rows rest on judgment alone, and their evidence column says so.

## Sources

- **Operator and school FAQs (question lists read in full):** Skynomad, Benlo, Freedom Parapente, Yumping, Southern Skies, Flybubble, Paragliding San Francisco, Wills Wing, Lookout, Manawa, Jocky Sanderson, CheckYeti help centre.
- **Federations:** USHPA, BHPA, SAHPA, FAI/CIVL and HPAC. DHV and FFVL come in only through test reports and statistics.
- **Pilot media:** XC Mag, the Flybubble blog, Ozone Infozone, 406 Paragliding, paragliding.gr.
- **Community:** r/paragliding thread titles, one r/freeflight thread, a GummySearch summary of r/freeflight, and UK club forums.
- **Wikipedia:** the structure of the Paragliding article, plus related articles.
- **Search-result signals:** how many publishers have a dedicated page for a question (including content farms, e.g. "how dangerous is paragliding"), and news spikes such as the 2025 cloud-suck story.

## Strongest clusters

1. **Safety and risk:** is it safe, how dangerous is it, how many people die, is tandem safe.
2. **Getting started:** licence, learning time, age, weight, fitness, fear of heights.
3. **Cost:** the overall question, then tandem, lessons and gear.
4. **Comparisons:** paragliding vs hang gliding, parasailing, skydiving and paramotor.
5. **Tandem logistics:** what to wear, how long it lasts, experience needed, weather.
6. **How high, far and fast:** these lead into the records pages.
7. **Questions active pilots ask:** EN ratings and first wing, collapses, wing lifespan, weather apps, cloud suck.

Some topics have low search demand but high strategic value: Kenya, Bir Billing, the Himalayan season, the IPPI card, and podcasts.

## Caveats

- **No volume data was available.** The search tool never showed "People also ask" boxes, so demand is inferred from how many independent sources answer each question. That method undercounts niche pilot questions and overcounts what operators like to answer.
- **No YouTube view counts.** The vidIQ connector was not used, because it may spend credits.
- **Missing sources:** Reddit, BHPA and DHV pages could not be fetched directly.
- **Records disagree:** Wikipedia and the FAI CIVL page differ. Check every record and statistic at the primary source.
