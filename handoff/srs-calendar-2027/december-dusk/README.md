# December 2027: SRS_6_T6_106 as a season-finale sunset (website chat 30, 24 Nov 2026)

The owner: "use this for december" (https://paraglidingatlas.com/media-srs-tapalpa-2026.html#SRS_6_T6_106).
- dec_grade.py: the blue daytime sky re-lit as a sunset (indigo, magenta, orange low down), the clouds peach in the
  light and violet in shade, the pod, pilot and lines as silhouettes; the wing keeps its own colours, highlights held
  back (the owner found November's wing over-exposed). `python3 dec_grade.py DIR` with DIR/src.jpg (2400px as JPG).
- build.js: the builder with the tower layout taking a class; "dk" sets the month name smaller (so it clears the pod)
  and lightens the bottom shade (so the orange horizon shows). Entry: {file:'13-december',type:'tower',cls:'dk',m:12,img:'o_dec.jpg',pos:'100% 50%'}.
- pages/13-december-1200.webp and -2400.webp: the finished page.
