How we work with sponsors

The trust is the product

Pilots come here because the knowledge is honest. That is what makes a mention here worth something, so it is protected in every partnership.

  1. Sponsored episodes and segments are clearly marked and disclosed at the start of the episode.
  2. We only partner with brands and products we believe in and that align with our values.
  3. Editorial independence stays with us. When we review a product, we say if it was provided free and disclose any relationship with the manufacturer.
  4. Affiliate relationships are disclosed in the episode description.

The full policy is on our Safety & Disclosure page.
