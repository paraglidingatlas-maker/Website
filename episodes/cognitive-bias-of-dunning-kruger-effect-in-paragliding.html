A short extract in which Beni Kalin and Heli Schrempf discuss how flying constantly can breed overconfidence rather than guard against it, particularly in speed flying where the air is smooth and the ground is close. They suggest a break of a few months is often useful, because coming back and finding it fast again is what keeps the risk visible.
