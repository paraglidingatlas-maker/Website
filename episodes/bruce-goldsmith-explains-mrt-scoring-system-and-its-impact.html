Bruce Goldsmith proposes multi radius turnpoints, giving lighter pilots a larger turnpoint cylinder to cancel out the performance advantage that heavier pilots carry. He argues the current format has narrowed the decisions available inside a task, producing huge gaggles racing fast along ridges, and that drag reducing measures only ever address a narrow band of pilot weights.
