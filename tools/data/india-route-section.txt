  <details class="v4-fold v4t-rintro"><summary>How the days go</summary><p>One launch, one landing field, and a front range that works for tens of kilometres either way. The tour follows the conditions rather than a schedule; these are the shapes the days tend to take.</p></details>
  <ul class="iroute-list">
    <li class="iroute-item">
      <h3>Bir to Dharamshala and back</h3>
      <span class="iroute-km">West, 80 to 90 km out and return</span>
      <details class="v4-fold v4t-rfold"><summary>More</summary><p>The standard day, and the line Eddie Colfox would send a newer pilot on: west along the front range to Dharamshala and home, with big fields to land in and easy retrieves the whole way. In his telling, pilots were already flying it out and back in 1988. <a href="../episodes/navigating-india-eddie-colfox.html#c4">Hear him on it</a>.</p></details>
    </li>
    <li class="iroute-item is-east">
      <h3>Add 360 on the way home</h3>
      <span class="iroute-km">East, the bigger day</span>
      <details class="v4-fold v4t-rfold"><summary>More</summary><p>With Dharamshala done, his next step is to turn back east past the takeoff to 360 and then home again. Same front range, same landing options, more of it.</p></details>
    </li>
    <li class="iroute-item is-over">
      <h3>Over the back</h3>
      <span class="iroute-km">North, not a first week line</span>
      <details class="v4-fold v4t-rfold"><summary>More</summary><p>Behind Billing is a spine, and past it the big mountains: Barabangal, the run to Manali and beyond. Jigish Gohil's test for going is a couple of hundred hours, an SIV course, and the kit and fitness for a night out and a long walk. <a href="../episodes/navigating-india-jigish-gohil.html#c6">His test in full</a>.</p></details>
