What happens to this

This form has no database behind it. Pressing send opens your own email app with the message written out, and you send it yourself. Nothing is posted to us or to any form service on the way, so there is no third party holding your details.

If your device has no email app set up, the button will tell you, and you can copy the text or use WhatsApp instead.

What we do with it

We read it and reply, usually within a couple of days. We do not add you to a mailing list from an enquiry, and we do not pass your details to anyone. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
