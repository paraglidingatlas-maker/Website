Flying As A Way Of Seeing The World

At Paragliding Atlas, I believe our sport isn't just about flying. It's about experiencing the world from a perspective few dare to explore: embracing the wind, mastering the skies, and discovering the untold stories of the clouds and the lands beneath them.

I am driven by a single passion: to turn every flying holiday into a series of unforgettable moments, delivered with genuine hospitality and the highest safety standards I can build.

Flying can feel like love at first sight, and that's exactly what happened to me. As a child, I took my first tandem flight in the foothills of the Himalayas.

  1. Childhood

    I take my first tandem paragliding flight in the foothills of the Himalayas. The obsession begins.

  2. 2023

    I found Paragliding Atlas, aiming to build immersive flying experiences beyond a quick tandem flight.

  3. Today

    Guiding expeditions across Kenya, the Himalayas, Peru, and Kazakhstan, and telling the stories behind them on the Paragliding Atlas podcast.
